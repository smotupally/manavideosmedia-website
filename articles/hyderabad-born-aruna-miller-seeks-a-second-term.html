🗳️ Hyderabad-born Aruna Miller seeks a second term!

Hyderabad-born Aruna Miller, Maryland's lieutenant governor, is seeking a second term in Tuesday's November 3 midterm elections, running alongside Governor Wes Moore.

• Born in Hyderabad in November 1964, Miller spent her first seven years in the city; her parents hail from Andhra Pradesh's Krishna district. • In 2022 she made history as the first woman of colour and immigrant elected to statewide office in Maryland — and the first South Asian woman lieutenant governor in the United States. • The Democratic Moore-Miller ticket faces the Republican ticket of Dan Cox and Rob Krop; Maryland votes on Tuesday, November 3. • Her second-term agenda: improving road safety, tackling the opioid crisis, and preparing young people for STEM careers. • In her words: "Every bit of my Telugu identity is part of my DNA."
