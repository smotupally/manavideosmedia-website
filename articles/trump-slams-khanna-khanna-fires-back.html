🔥 Trump slams Khanna — Khanna fires back!

President Donald Trump attacked Indian American Congressman Ro Khanna repeatedly on Friday, October 30, over Khanna's criticism of the Iran war's cost — calling him a "lightweight" at the White House and a "total dope" at a midterm rally in Daytona, Florida.

• The flashpoint: Khanna's estimate that the Iran war cost $47 billion — money he says should go to jobs and childcare for Americans instead. • Khanna's reply on X: "Thanks for the 4 shoutouts today, Mr. President. I'll be on Fox this Sunday" to discuss the Iran failure and rising gas and food prices. • What's next: Americans vote in the midterm elections on Tuesday, November 3.

ట్రంప్ విమర్శలకు ఖన్నా గట్టిగా బదులిచ్చారు. ఇరాన్ యుద్ధ ఖర్చుపై ఆయన చేసిన విమర్శలే ఈ మాటల యుద్ధానికి కారణం.
