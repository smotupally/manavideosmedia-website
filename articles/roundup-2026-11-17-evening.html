🇺🇸 USA Telugu News Roundup — Tuesday Evening, Nov 17

• 1️⃣ Court blocks student-visa cap — a Boston federal judge struck down the proposal to cap international students' stay at four years. The Duration of Status policy stays — students can remain for the length of their programme. The ruling is relief for about 331,000 Indian students in the US.

• 2️⃣ Green-card rules: big overhaul proposed — the Labor Department has proposed ending the Schedule A fast-track for nurses and physical therapists, a minimum 90 days of online recruitment, and a 2-year layoff look-back for employers. This is a proposal only — nothing changes yet. Public comments are due Jan 19, 2027, and filings before Feb 16, 2027 would be processed under the old rules.

• 3️⃣ Quad leaders meet in Manila — President Trump, PM Modi, PM Albanese and PM Takaichi met in Manila and issued a joint statement. A first Quad Business Leaders' Forum is planned for early 2027, and Trump said over $85M will go to trusted AI ecosystems.

• 4️⃣ 81.5M to travel for Thanksgiving — AAA expects 81.5 million Americans to travel Nov 24–30. 72.9 million by car, 5.96 million by air. Average domestic round-trip airfare is about $800. Worst traffic: Tue Nov 24 and Wed Nov 25, 2–8 PM.
