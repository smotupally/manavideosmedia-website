Pichai Tops Hurun Global Indians List Nadella Second

ఎన్నారై వార్తలు
2026-11-13•2 min read•By Mana Videos Media

Pichai Tops Hurun Global Indians List; Nadella Second

Sundar Pichai, CEO of Alphabet, tops the HSBC Hurun Global Indians List 2026, leading a company valued at $4.2 trillion. Satya Nadella, Microsoft chairman and CEO, ranks second at $2.6 trillion. Nadella is from Hyderabad.

Micron chief Sanjay Mehrotra is third at $1.4 trillion, followed by YouTube CEO Neal Mohan and Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian. Together, the top five lead companies worth $9.1 trillion — about 61% of the list's total value.

The list features 236 Indian-origin leaders heading 200 companies headquartered outside India, with a combined value of $15 trillion — up 53% from $9.9 trillion in the first edition in 2024. The companies employ 5.3 million people and generate $2.5 trillion in revenue. The list was released on November 12 by HSBC India and Hurun India.
