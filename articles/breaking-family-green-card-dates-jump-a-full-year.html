BREAKING: Family Green Card Dates Jump a Full Year! 🇺🇸

The US State Department's November 2026 Visa Bulletin brings good news for Indian families: F1, F2B and F3 final action dates advance by about a year. F2A moves up one month, while F4 stays unchanged (Dec 15, 2006). Employment-based dates barely move — EB-2 India stays frozen at Nov 1, 2013, and EB-3 advances just 21 days to Jan 22, 2014.

Check your priority date against the new bulletin!
