💰 Desi donors tilt 4-to-1 to Republicans ahead of the US midterms!

Indian-American donors have given $15.8 million to Republican-linked PACs since 2025, versus $3.7 million to Democrats — a 4-to-1 funding edge ahead of the November 3 midterms, per a Hindustan Times analysis of FEC data.

Top donors: venture capitalist Asha Jadeja Motwani — $5.9 million; entrepreneur Michelle D'Souza — more than $4 million.
