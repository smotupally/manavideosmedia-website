🏛️ Hyderabad roots, Illinois win! Nabeela Syed wins State Senate seat!

At 27, Nabeela Syed has flipped a Republican-held Illinois State Senate seat, winning District 26 in the Chicago northwest suburbs with about 57% of the vote against incumbent Darby Hills.

• Syed, a Democrat, was born and raised in Illinois to parents who moved to the United States from Hyderabad in the 1980s. She made history in 2022 when she was elected to the Illinois House at just 23 — one of the youngest lawmakers in the state. • Her win flips a seat previously held by Republicans and marks another milestone in her fast-rising political career. She announced her victory on Instagram, writing: "I am so grateful to every single voter in the 26th Senate District who made this victory possible." • During her time in the House, Syed focused on consumer protection, health care and voting access. Her Senate campaign took up the impact of AI data centers on local communities — electricity costs, taxes and the environment. • The result came on a big Democratic night across the country in the November 3 midterm elections, with Indian Americans winning key races in several states.
