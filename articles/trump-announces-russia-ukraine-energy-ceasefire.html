⚡ TRUMP ANNOUNCES RUSSIA-UKRAINE ENERGY CEASEFIRE!

Oct 11, 2026 — President Trump announced on Truth Social Sunday that Russia and Ukraine have agreed to an "energy ceasefire" effective immediately — halting strikes on each other's energy infrastructure: oil refineries, power plants and electricity networks.

🛢️ Context: the announcement follows Friday's controversial US-Russia diesel deal — 300,000 tons immediately, 500,000 in November, 1M+ after — with temporary US sanctions relief, aimed at bringing down diesel prices, currently $6.28/gallon nationally.

🇺🇦 Kyiv's response: President Zelensky called the announcement "news" to him, but said Ukraine would agree if Russia does. He reiterated Ukraine will stop hitting Russian refineries if Moscow stops striking Ukraine's power grid — and offered to hold elections if Russia agrees to a 60-day total ceasefire.

⏳ Honest note: at announcement time, neither Kyiv nor Moscow had publicly confirmed the deal. A similar Trump claim in September was never confirmed by Moscow.

🇮🇳🇺🇸 Why it matters for India-US: the energy thread runs through everything — India's Russian oil imports, the Modi-Trump call's sanctions concerns, and this week's Iran-oil sanctions. Mana Videos Media keeps tracking.
