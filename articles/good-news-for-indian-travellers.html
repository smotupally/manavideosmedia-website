🛂 GOOD NEWS FOR INDIAN TRAVELLERS! 🇮🇳✈️

Oct 3, 2026 — The US State Department released a revised list on Friday naming 50 COUNTRIES whose B1/B2 (tourist/business) visa applicants must post bonds of up to $20,000 — and INDIA IS EXEMPT! ✅

💰 Bonds of $10,000 / $15,000 / $20,000 (set by the consular officer at the interview) 🇮🇳 Indian passport holders — no bond needed 🇧🇩🇧🇹🇳🇵 Bangladesh, Bhutan and Nepal ARE on the bond list ⚖️ Rule runs under INA Section 221(g)(3), based on DHS overstay data; final rule issued Aug 3, 2026 ⚠️ Bond is filed via Form I-352 + Pay.gov ONLY after the consular officer asks — and paying does NOT guarantee a visa
