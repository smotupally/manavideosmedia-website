⚖️ Judge blocks threats against election officials!

A federal judge has temporarily barred the Justice Department from threatening state and local election officials over alleged noncitizen voting, just days before the November 3 midterms.

• The order: U.S. District Judge Trina Thompson (Northern District of California) issued a temporary restraining order on Friday, October 30, in a lawsuit brought by election workers' unions and other groups. • What it stops: demanding that officials remove voters from statewide rolls, refuse ballots to specific voters, or segregate midterm ballots based on an administration list of alleged non-citizen voters. • What's next: a preliminary-injunction hearing is set for November 13. This is a temporary order, not a final ruling — and the Justice Department had no immediate comment. • Why it matters: the judge wrote that the public has a paramount interest in election officials being able to carry out their work under federal law and election procedure, with Election Day only days away.
