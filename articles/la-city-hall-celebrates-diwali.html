LA City Hall Celebrates Diwali

The Los Angeles City Council unanimously adopted a special resolution recognising Diwali and the contributions of South Asian communities to the city. Mayor-elect Nithya Raman hosted the celebration at City Hall on November 13, with India's Consul General K. J. Srinivasa, council members and hundreds of Indian-American community members in attendance.

The evening featured Carnatic music, tabla, Indian classical dance and Bhangra. Raman also presented a Certificate of Recognition to Dr Srinivasa for his work strengthening India–Los Angeles ties, including cooperation ahead of the 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games.

Raman won the mayoral election on November 3 and will be Los Angeles' first South Asian mayor when she takes office on December 14.
