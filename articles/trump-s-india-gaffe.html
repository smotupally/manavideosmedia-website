🗳️ TRUMP'S INDIA GAFFE! 🇮🇳

Oct 5, 2026 — In a Truth Social post, US President Donald Trump claimed "125 million people voted today in the first round of India's Presidential Election" — praising how the results came out the same day.

⚠️ The catch: India held NO such election — India's President is chosen by an electoral college, not a popular vote. The 125-million figure matches BRAZIL's first-round presidential election held Sunday — Flávio Bolsonaro led incumbent Lula 47.24% to 44.91%, so a runoff is set for October 25.

✅ Trump later corrected the post.
