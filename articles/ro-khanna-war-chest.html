Ro Khanna Builds $16.9M War Chest Amid 2028 Senate Buzz

రో ఖన్నా నిధుల సేకరణలో దూసుకుపోతున్నారు
Oct 16, 20263 min readBy Mana Videos Media
Politics

WASHINGTON — Rep. Ro Khanna's latest fundraising filings show a $16.9 million war chest, a figure that has set political observers in California buzzing about the Indian-American congressman's ambitions ahead of 2028.

Khanna, who represents a Silicon Valley district in the House, has long been mentioned as a potential candidate for higher office. The size of his fundraising haul — built largely from small-dollar donors alongside tech-industry supporters — gives him unusual financial flexibility for whatever he decides next, whether that is a Senate run or a continued leadership role in the House.

For the Indian-American community, Khanna remains one of the most visible desi faces in national politics. His fundraisers routinely draw Telugu and Indian-American donors from Texas and California alike, and community leaders say his prominence has inspired a generation of young South Asian Americans to consider public service.

Political analysts caution that a large war chest does not by itself signal a campaign. Incumbents in safe seats often build reserves as a show of strength, and Khanna has not announced any plans beyond his current role. Still, in a state where statewide races routinely cost tens of millions, starting with nearly $17 million on hand is a position few challengers can match.

Whatever comes next, Khanna's team says the focus remains on his House work — technology policy, manufacturing, and economic issues that resonate well beyond his district. The 2028 speculation, for now, stays speculation.
