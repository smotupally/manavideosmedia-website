Visa rule comments close next week!

⏰ Visa rule comments close next week!

Three proposals from the US Department of Homeland Security are open for public comments — and the deadlines fall on November 9, 10 and 13. None of them is law yet.

• Nov 9 — OPT fees: a proposed $70,000 fee before a student's first OPT recommendation and $30,000 for each later one. The school is the legal payer and may pass the cost on. • Nov 10 — End of the 60-day grace period: the proposal would remove the up-to-60-day window H-1B, L-1, O-1, TN and E workers and their dependants get after a job ends. The grace period still applies today. • Nov 13 — No deference to past approvals: USCIS would stop deferring to an earlier approval on the same facts when reviewing an extension, which could mean more requests for evidence.

About 27,000 comments have already been filed on the grace-period proposal, 4,793 on OPT fees and about 400 on the deference rule (as of Nov 1). India-born workers received about 71% of the roughly 399,000 H-1B petitions approved in FY2024.
