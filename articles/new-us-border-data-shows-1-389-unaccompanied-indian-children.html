New US border data shows 1,389 unaccompanied Indian children have been taken into custody since 2023.

వీసా & ఇమ్మిగ్రేషన్ వార్తలు
2026-11-23•2 min read•By Mana Videos Media

New US border data shows 1,389 unaccompanied Indian children have been taken into custody since 2023.

The yearly count has fallen sharply: 730 children were found in fiscal year 2023, then 517 in 2024, 91 in 2025, and 51 in 2026. In October 2026 — the first month of fiscal year 2027 — only 2 were found.

None of the 1,389 children has been sent back to India so far. In fiscal year 2026, US authorities recorded 30,528 encounters with Indian nationals, placing India third among nationalities, behind Mexico and Canada.
