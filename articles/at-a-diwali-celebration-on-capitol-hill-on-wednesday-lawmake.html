At a Diwali celebration on Capitol Hill on Wednesday, lawmakers from both parties joined Indian Americans to condemn hos

రాజకీయ వార్తలు
2026-11-19•2 min read•By Mana Videos Media

At a Diwali celebration on Capitol Hill on Wednesday, lawmakers from both parties joined Indian Americans to condemn hostility toward the community and call for religious freedom.

Thirteen members of Congress, led by Reps. Pramila Jayapal, Raja Krishnamoorthi and Grace Meng, have asked the Justice Department and the FBI to investigate whether the November 7 disruption of Seattle's Diwali festival — when two men entered the Seattle Center Armory with signs carrying racial slurs — violated federal civil rights laws. The lawmakers have asked for a briefing by December 4. The Justice Department has not publicly responded.

Florida Congresswoman-elect Pia Dandiya, the first South Asian American elected to represent Florida in Congress, told young Indian American girls at the celebration that there is 'no limit' to what they can accomplish.
