🕯️ Telugu youth Rishi Reddy, 22, found dead in Ohio

Rishi Reddy, 22, from Ramannapet village in Mallial mandal of Jagtial district, Telangana, was found dead under suspicious circumstances in his hostel room in Ohio. His friends informed his family. He was the only son of his parents.

The cause of death is not yet known, and more details are still awaited.
