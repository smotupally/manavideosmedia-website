🌍🇮🇳 RUBIO TO VISIT INDIA — OCT 17–22

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio will travel to Mumbai and Jamnagar, India from October 17 to 22, the State Department announced Tuesday. Energy cooperation and trade top the agenda — he meets senior Indian officials and business leaders and tours Reliance's Jamnagar refinery, the world's largest single-site plant at 1.4 million barrels a day. He visits Samarkand, Uzbekistan first. This is his second India visit since May.
