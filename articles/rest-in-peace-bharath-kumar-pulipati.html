Rest in peace, Bharath Kumar Pulipati.

ఎన్నారై వార్తలు
2026-10-152 min readBy Mana Videos Media

Rest in peace, Bharath Kumar Pulipati.

A Telugu student from Nellore who came to America in 2021 with big dreams lost his four-year battle with advanced liver cancer in Houston on Oct 14.

From his hospital bed at MD Anderson, his only wish was to return home to Nellore in his final days — a wish that, sadly, remained unfulfilled. His family is now working to bring his mortal remains home.

నాలుగేళ్లు క్యాన్సర్‌తో పోరాడిన భరత్ కుమార్ పులిపాటి అక్టోబర్ 14న హ్యూస్టన్‌లో తుదిశ్వాస విడిచారు. ఆయన ఆత్మకు శాంతి కలగాలి.

His sister Yamini's GoFundMe fundraiser 'Support Bharath and Family in This Crisis' is supporting the family through this difficult time.
