Ramaswamy trails in two new Ohio polls!

రాజకీయ వార్తలు
2026-10-30•2 min read•By Mana Videos Media
Share this story: WhatsApp X Facebook

🗳️ Ramaswamy trails in two new Ohio polls!

Two new polls put Democrat Amy Acton ahead of Republican Vivek Ramaswamy in Ohio's governor race, just days before the November 3 vote.

• Suffolk University / USA TODAY Network Ohio poll (500 likely voters, Oct 21–26): Acton 48%, Ramaswamy 44%, Libertarian Don Kissick 4%, 3% undecided — narrower than September's 50–41 lead for Acton. • A Republican internal poll obtained by Axios (800 likely voters, Oct 23–26): Acton 50%, Ramaswamy 42% — widening from a 47–45 Acton lead earlier in October. • Ramaswamy's $4 million ad featuring Acton's estranged mother appears to have backfired: 72% of likely voters had seen or heard it, but fewer than 1% said it moved them to Ramaswamy, while 2% moved from Ramaswamy to Acton. The ad has now stopped airing — Ramaswamy said Oct 29 it "ran to completion."

If elected, Ramaswamy would be Ohio's first Indian-American governor.
