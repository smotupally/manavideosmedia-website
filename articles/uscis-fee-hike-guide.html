USCIS Fee Hike: What Telugu Tech Workers Need to Know

వీసా ఫీజుల పెంపు — తెలుగు టెక్ ఉద్యోగులకు సూచనలు
Oct 16, 20264 min readBy Mana Videos Media
Visa & Immigration

HOUSTON, TX — The new USCIS fee schedule now in effect this quarter raises costs across nearly every immigration benefit, and attorneys say Telugu tech workers — one of the largest H-1B communities in the country — should review their upcoming filings now to avoid surprises.

The increases touch the forms most familiar to H-1B families: employment-based petitions, adjustment of status applications, employment authorization renewals, and dependent filings all carry higher price tags than last year. Attorneys note that premium processing fees, already a significant cost for many workers, have also been adjusted upward.

For workers mid-process on green cards, the timing matters. "If your employer is about to file an I-140 or you are preparing an I-485, talk to your attorney about filing before any additional adjustments," said one Dallas-based immigration lawyer who advises many Telugu clients. Filing fees are generally locked to the date USCIS receives the application, not the date it is prepared.

Dependents are not spared. Families filing for H-4 spouses or children should budget for the higher per-person costs, especially if renewals for the whole family fall in the same year. Attorneys recommend that workers ask their employers early about which fees the company covers — policies vary widely, and some employers reimburse only the principal applicant's costs.

The practical advice from attorneys is straightforward: keep your documents organized, start renewals earlier than you think you need to, and set aside a filing-fee buffer. Fee-related rejections — sending the wrong amount with a form — remain one of the most common and most avoidable reasons applications get bounced back.
