Dulquer Salmaan is in Dallas today!

Dulquer Salmaan and Satvika Veeravalli are meeting fans at the 'Sky-High Blockbuster' success meet for Aakasamlo Oka Tara at Bharat Bhavan (530 W Lyndon B Johnson Fwy) in Irving, Texas, today (Saturday, November 21) at 12:00 PM CST.

The film, directed by Pavan Sadineni, opened in theatres worldwide on November 20 and has been getting praise from audiences and critics. Dulquer's three-city US tour has already stopped in San Francisco (November 19) and Chicago (November 20) — Dallas is the final stop. Producer Anil Sunkara is the special invitee. The event is presented by Ultra Entertainments with Saregama Cinemas.
