Green card rules head for first overhaul since 2005!

వీసా & ఇమ్మిగ్రేషన్ వార్తలు

📰 Green card rules head for first overhaul since 2005!

Big news for everyone on the employment green card path: the White House has cleared a U.S. Labor Department plan to rewrite PERM — the labor certification that is the first step when an employer sponsors a worker for a green card (EB-2 / EB-3). It would be the first comprehensive update to the PERM rules since 2005.

What we know so far: • White House (OIRA) review of the proposed rule was completed Oct 23, 2026 • It would revise employer recruitment standards, strengthen layoff-related protections for U.S. workers, and update non-discrimination and record-keeping rules • Next step: publication in the Federal Register, then a public comment period (expected 30 or 60 days)

Important: this is a PROPOSED rule, not yet in effect. The full text has not been published yet, so the exact impact will only be clear once it is out. Nothing changes for current PERM cases today.
