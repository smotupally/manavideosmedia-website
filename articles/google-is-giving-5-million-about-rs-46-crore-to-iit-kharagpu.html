Google is giving 5 million (about Rs 46 crore) to IIT Kharagpur to power advanced AI research.

కమ్యూనిటీ వార్తలు
2026-11-22•2 min read•By Mana Videos Media

Google is giving $5 million (about Rs 46 crore) to IIT Kharagpur to power advanced AI research.

The funding was announced during the institute's 75th anniversary celebrations on November 21. Google CEO Sundar Pichai — an IIT Kharagpur alumnus — played a key role in making it happen.

The money will support three things: advanced AI research, AI education and training for students and faculty, and building AI capacity on campus. The research will cover Indic languages, agriculture, materials science and educational technology.

Pichai said IIT Kharagpur is doing groundbreaking work in Indic languages, agriculture and education, and that Google is excited to help accelerate it.

Fellow alumnus Jyoti Chatterjee pledged another $5 million as a seed endowment for a next-generation data initiative.
