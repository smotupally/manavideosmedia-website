Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico is Miss Universe 2026!

కమ్యూనిటీ వార్తలు
2026-11-25•2 min read•By Mana Videos Media

Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico is Miss Universe 2026!

The 27-year-old psychologist and social worker was crowned on Tuesday night, November 24, at the 75th Miss Universe pageant in San Juan, Puerto Rico — winning on home soil in front of a home crowd.

Barreto is the first mother — and the first married woman — to win Miss Universe, three years after the pageant lifted its ban on mothers and married contestants. She has a young daughter, Penelope. She was crowned by last year's winner, Fátima Bosch of Mexico, and received the new crown 'The Radiance of an Eternal Legacy', set with 3,150 diamonds in 18-karat white gold.

She was chosen from 116 contestants from around the world. Melissa Núñez of the Dominican Republic was first runner-up, and Clara Vegas of Venezuela was second runner-up.

India's Kaziah Liz Mejo, a 19-year-old law student from Kerala, reached the Top 30 — the first woman from Kerala to win the Miss Universe India title and represent India at the pageant.

Next year's Miss Universe will be held in Costa Rica.
