US ADMIRAL PAPARO IN INDIA — 6-DAY DEFENCE VISIT!

ఎన్నారై వార్తలు

⚓ US ADMIRAL PAPARO IN INDIA — 6-DAY DEFENCE VISIT! 🇮🇳🇺🇸

Oct 7, 2026 — Admiral Samuel Paparo, Commander of the U.S. Indo-Pacific Command, is on a six-day official visit to India (Oct 3–8), meeting India's top defence leadership in New Delhi.

🤝 What happened: On Tuesday, Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh held talks with Admiral Paparo on deepening India-US defence and strategic cooperation, India's Ministry of Defence confirmed.

🌊 Also on the visit: Paparo met External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar on advancing the security partnership and maritime cooperation in the Indo-Pacific. US Ambassador Sergio Gor hosted a reception bringing Paparo together with CDS Gen N. S. Raja Subramani, Army Chief Gen Dhiraj Seth, and other senior officials — a showcase of the growing U.S.-India Major Defence Partnership.

🎯 Focus areas: emerging technology, maritime security, defence energy resilience, and regional strategic stability. Paparo commands ~400,000 military and civilian personnel — America's oldest and largest geographic combatant command. This is his second meeting with Singh this year, after the Shangri-La Dialogue in May.
