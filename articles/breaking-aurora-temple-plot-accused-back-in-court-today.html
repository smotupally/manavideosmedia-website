BREAKING: Aurora Temple Plot — Accused Back in Court Today

Alexandros Paporakis, 19, returns to a Cook County courtroom in Rolling Meadows today, Monday, November 9, for a formal indictment hearing in the alleged plot to attack the Sri Venkateswara Swami (Balaji) Temple in Aurora, Illinois. Formal charges will be read to move the case forward.

Paporakis was arrested on October 11, charged with making a terrorist threat and unlawfully possessing a rifle with a barrel shorter than 16 inches. Police say he drove to the temple on October 11 and tried to open fire, but his rifle jammed before a single round was fired. A co-worker who overheard him talking about the failed attempt alerted police. Investigators later found firearms, ammunition, and an eight-page manifesto at his home.

A judge ordered him held in custody, ruling him a clear threat to public safety. His new private attorney has filed a motion for a speedy trial. Congressman Raja Krishnamoorthi and other lawmakers continue to push for federal hate-crime charges. The charges are allegations, and the case is pending.
