🌆 Saturday Evening, Oct 24 — USA Telugu News Roundup 🇺🇸

• Infosys has disclosed a US Department of Justice investigation into how it classified certain H-1B visa holders in immigration documents, in its financial statements for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The company says it is in discussions with the DOJ and cannot yet predict the outcome. In 2013, Infosys paid $34 million to settle an earlier US immigration probe.

• University of Houston student Vasant Bhatt, 21, says conservative outlet Campus Reform ended his correspondent role after he voted for Democrat James Talarico over Republican Ken Paxton in the Texas Senate race, protesting anti-Indian bigotry. Campus Reform has not publicly responded, and his author profile has been removed from its site.

• btin.co.in / IANS)

• Indian-American donors have given Republican-linked PACs $15.8 million since 2025, versus $3.7 million to Democrats — a 4-to-1 edge ahead of the November 3 midterms, per FEC data reported by Hindustan Times. Top donor: venture capitalist Asha Jadeja Motwani, $5.9 million.

• Former US Transportation Secretary Pete Buttigieg will campaign with Indian-American Democrat Pia Dandiya at a canvassing launch in Plantation, Florida, on Monday, October 26. Her race in Florida's 22nd District has moved from Lean Republican to Toss-up. Florida early voting runs October 24–31; Election Day is November 3.
