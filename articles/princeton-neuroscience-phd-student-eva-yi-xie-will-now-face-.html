Princeton neuroscience PhD student Eva Yi Xie will now face an immigration judge on Thursday, December 3 — more than a y

వీసా & ఇమ్మిగ్రేషన్ వార్తలు
2026-11-26•2 min read•By Mana Videos Media

Princeton neuroscience PhD student Eva Yi Xie will now face an immigration judge on Thursday, December 3 — more than a year earlier than the January 18, 2028 date in her case.

Xie, 24, was detained by ICE at Reagan National Airport on November 19, after presenting her research at a neuroscience conference in Washington, D.C. Court filings show she was arrested under an administrative warrant, and the government has now asked a federal judge to reject her habeas corpus petition seeking release.

The government argues that a pending asylum application does not protect her from detention, and says it would promptly arrange a bond hearing if she requests one. Her lawyers say the arrest was unlawful — no warrant was shown before the arrest, and she is not a flight risk. She has no criminal history, and her work permit was approved three times while her asylum case is pending. She remains held at the Caroline Detention Facility in Virginia. Her lawyers must reply to the government's filing by Monday at 5 p.m.
