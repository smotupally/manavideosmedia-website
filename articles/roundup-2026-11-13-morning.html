USA Telugu News Roundup — Friday morning, Nov 13, 2026

• 1. Indian-American astronaut Sunita "Suni" Williams led New York City's 107th Veterans Day Parade as Grand Marshal on Wednesday, marching with more than 20,000 people up Fifth Avenue. The retired Navy captain spent 608 days in space across three missions and holds the record for the most spacewalks by a woman — nine, totalling 62 hours.

• 2. US envoys Witkoff and Kushner briefed President Zelenskyy in Dublin late Thursday after their Moscow meeting with President Putin. Zelenskyy said the prisoner exchange agreed in Abu Dhabi would be the largest of the war — "a real result" — and the envoys said Europeans will join the next round of talks, next week. Moscow has not confirmed European participation.

• 3. US jobless claims rose 9,000 to 200,000 last week, above the 195,000 forecast, while continuing claims fell to 1.697 million. Existing home sales fell 5.5% in October to a 3.75 million annual rate — the lowest since August 2010 — even as the median price hit a record for October at $421,900, with the 30-year mortgage rate at 7.11%.

• 4. Nvidia shares fell again Thursday, dipping below $150 before closing at $166.19, down 4.34%. The week's market-value decline reached $550 billion, and the stock is down more than 30% from its October 6 record. Retail investors bought the dip in record size. Q3 results are due November 17.
