NYC Mayor-elect Zohran Mamdani visited the Om Shakti Temple in Queens on November 15 and called for Hindu Americans to be able to worship in peace and safety, after the foiled mass-shooting plot at a Hindu temple in Aurora, Illinois.

Speaking to the gathering at the temple, Mamdani said protecting religious communities and letting people worship without fear is a collective responsibility. "I know I am standing here not that long since an attempted mass shooting at a Hindu temple in Illinois. We have the responsibility to ensure Hindu-Americans can worship in peace and safety," he said.

The Aurora plot was foiled on October 11, when 19-year-old Alexandros Paporakis was arrested after his rifle jammed at the Sri Venkateswara Swami (Balaji) Temple. Congressman Raja Krishnamoorthi was inside the temple with his family at the time. 105 members of Congress have urged the FBI to open a federal hate-crime investigation.
