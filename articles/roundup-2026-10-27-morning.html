🇺🇸 USA Telugu News Roundup — Tuesday, October 27, 2026 (Morning) ☀️

• Today's top stories for Telugu people in America:

• 1️⃣ 256 lawmakers back Indian American Heritage Month — Raja Krishnamoorthi's resolution to designate July as Indian American Heritage Month now has 256 cosponsors in the House, honouring the 1946 Luce-Celler Act that first opened US citizenship to people of Indian descent.

• 2️⃣ India-US trade deal enters final fine-tuning — Minister Piyush Goyal says most discussions are concluded, and the White House calls the talks "productive."

• 3️⃣ Karthik Shekhar wins $200,000 Philomathia Prize — the UC Berkeley professor's bioelectricity research earns one of the university's top early-career honours. Prize presented Nov 5.

• 4️⃣ Soham Basanwar wins three computer science honours — the UIC junior finished first in the senior and US national categories at the International Computer Science Competition, top 1% of 4,300 entrants.

• 5️⃣ Anti-Indian rhetoric risks Republican votes — with the Nov 3 midterms a week away, a new report finds anti-South Asian slurs online up 109%, and voters weighing visa policy and health costs.
