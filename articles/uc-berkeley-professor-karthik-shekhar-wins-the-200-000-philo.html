UC Berkeley professor Karthik Shekhar wins the 200,000 Philomathia Prize!

🏆 UC Berkeley professor Karthik Shekhar wins the $200,000 Philomathia Prize!

Karthik Shekhar, assistant professor of chemical and biomolecular engineering at UC Berkeley, has won the 2026 Philomathia Prize for his research into bioelectricity — how electrical signals work in the body and brain.

The $200,000 award will support his collaborative research over three years. The prize will be formally presented on November 5 during the Philomathia Day symposium, "Bioelectricity at the Interface: Molecules, Membranes, and Models."
