A federal judge in Manhattan on Friday, November 20, sentenced Indian national Nikhil Gupta, 55, to 235 months — more than 19 years — in prison for a foiled murder-for-hire plot targeting Sikh separatist activist Gurpatwant Singh Pannun in New York.

Gupta pleaded guilty in February to murder-for-hire, conspiracy to commit murder-for-hire, and conspiracy to commit money laundering. Prosecutors said he agreed to pay $100,000 for the killing and handed $15,000 in cash to a purported hitman who was an undercover US Drug Enforcement Administration officer. Prosecutors had sought about 24 years in prison; Gupta's lawyers had asked for 12 years.

Judge Victor Marrero handed down the sentence in the Southern District of New York, with Pannun present in court. Gupta apologized in court, and Pannun said he was grateful to the US for prosecuting the case. Prosecutors said the plot was arranged with Vikash Yadav, an Indian government official who is also charged but is not in US custody. India has denied involvement in any plot against Pannun.
