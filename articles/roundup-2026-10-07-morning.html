🇺🇸 USA Telugu News Roundup — Wednesday Morning, Oct 7 ☀️

• Today's top stories for Telugu people in America:

• DOJ announces the LARGEST denaturalization drive in modern US history — citizenship-revocation cases filed against 40 people, including Indian-origin Balwinder Singh (55), accused of using two identities to naturalize in 2009. AG warns those who lied their way to citizenship will not be spared.

• New CCIA study: the proposed $103,265 H-1B fee could COST the US Treasury $32–38 billion over 10 years if filings drop 36–38%. The fee equals 78% of a median H-1B worker's annual pay — revenue projections assume zero drop in applications.

• US Olympic sprinter @kenny_bednarek backs India's 2036 Olympics bid! The IOC confirmed Tuesday that India is one of 7 nations in the race (with Germany, Qatar, Hungary, South Korea, South Africa, Turkey). Bednarek says he'll be in the stands if India hosts.

• venkateshdaggubati – Trivikram's 'AK 47' (Aadarsha Kutumbam House No: 47) hits theatres Oct 9! The action-comedy starring Venkatesh as Chittibabu opens with US premieres Oct 8 evening.
