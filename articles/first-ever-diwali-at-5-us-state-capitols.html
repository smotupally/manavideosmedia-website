First-ever Diwali at 5 US state capitols!

కమ్యూనిటీ వార్తలు

🪔 First-ever Diwali at 5 US state capitols!

For the first time, governors in five US states — Idaho, Nebraska, Montana, North Dakota and Washington — hosted Diwali celebrations at their state capitols and official residences, the Consulate General of India in Seattle said on Thursday.

• Washington Governor Bob Ferguson spoke at the Capitol rotunda in Olympia on November 4, noting the state had the fastest-growing Indian American population in the country over the past decade. • Idaho (Oct 16), Nebraska (Oct 20), Montana (Oct 26) and North Dakota (Oct 29) held their celebrations earlier; 22 cities across Washington held their own ceremonies, and nearly 200,000 Indian Americans live in the Greater Seattle area. • Oregon Governor Tina Kotek hosts the closing ceremony at the State Capitol in Salem on November 10.
