🏅 PRESIDENT TRUMP HONORS HYDERABAD'S SATYA NADELLA WITH AMERICA'S TOP TECH MEDAL!

Oct 9, 2026 — US President Donald Trump on Thursday presented the National Medal of Technology and Innovation — the highest US honor for technology achievement — to Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella at the "A New Golden Age" summit in Washington, DC.

🗣️ Trump's words: "For more than three decades, Satya Nadella has been a key figure in the transformation of Microsoft, revolutionizing the company and empowering businesses, students and consumers from all over the world." He called the honorees "national treasures."

🌍 Immigrant excellence: Five of the six tech leaders honored are immigrants — Nadella (India), Elon Musk (South Africa), Sergey Brin (Russia), Jensen Huang (Taiwan), and Lisa Su (Taiwan). Michael Dell (Texas) received the Technology medal alongside Nadella; Musk, Brin, Huang, and Su received the National Medal of Science.

⏰ Same-day contrast: the medal came just hours after VP JD Vance announced Microsoft's suspension from the PERM green-card program over alleged visa fraud — a story Mana Videos Media has covered all week.
