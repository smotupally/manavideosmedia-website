🔍 Help find Sreeja Koya — Telugu student missing in Boston

Sreeja Koya, 23, a student at the University of Massachusetts Boston from Nalgonda, Telangana, was last seen leaving her home near Edison Green in Dorchester at about 9:45 PM on Monday, November 2. She was reported missing on Tuesday, November 3, and Boston Police issued a public alert on Wednesday, November 4.

• She is described as 5'3"–5'5", thin build, with black hair, and was last seen wearing a black jacket and blue jeans. Police say she may be in the area of Carson Beach or Harbor Point; her family says she was last traced near Squantum Channel. • Anyone with information is asked to call 911 or Boston Police District C-11 (Dorchester) Detectives at (617) 343-4335. Anonymous tips can be given through the CrimeStoppers Tip Line at 1-800-494-TIPS.
