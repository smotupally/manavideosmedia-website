About Mana Videos Media

మన కథ — మన ప్రజల కోసం.

Help our people grow every day with the knowledge we give. మనం ఇచ్చే జ్ఞానంతో మన ప్రజలు ప్రతిరోజూ ఎదగాలి.

That is the core principle behind everything we publish. Mana Videos Media exists to give the Telugu diaspora in America genuine, honest, day-to-day information — in our language, in a way our people actually understand.

📰 Twice-Daily News Roundups

Every morning and evening at 6 AM / 6 PM CT, we publish a Telugu news roundup covering the USA stories that matter — immigration changes, community events, politics, and NRI life.

🗣️ Our Language First

Every reel is narrated in spoken Telugu — the way our people actually talk. No jargon, no stiffness. News that sounds like it comes from family.

✅ Verified Before Published

Every story ships with a verified source and publication date. If we can't confirm it's current, we don't run it. Trust is everything.

🤝 Community Over Clicks

We celebrate our students, our associations, our festivals, and our small businesses. Kid-achievement stories and community wins always have a home here.
